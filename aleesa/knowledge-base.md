## 7. Approach (how Graham works)

Six principles:
1. **Site** — finding the right opportunity first; location, context and
   potential shape everything that follows.
2. **Strategy** — understanding the market, location and target buyer before
   shaping the product.
3. **Design** — partnering with experienced architects and designers for
   lasting workmanship and places that remain relevant.
4. **Coordination** — bringing architects, designers, planners, engineers,
   builders and sales teams together as one team.
5. **Delivery** — from site identification through to handing over the keys;
   responsibility continues through construction and market delivery.
6. **People** — designing around how people ultimately live, not just the
   building as an asset.

Development journey: Opportunity → Site → Strategy → Design → Consultants →
Delivery → Market.
