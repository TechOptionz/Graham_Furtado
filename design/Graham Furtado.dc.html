About Graham

A property perspective built over decades.

Graham Furtado is a Queensland property developer and experienced real-estate professional with around three decades of industry experience. Through Furtado Property, he combines deep market knowledge with residential development experience, guiding projects from site opportunity and early development strategy through design, consultant coordination, construction and market delivery.

His current development story includes MIRA Living, a boutique 25-residence oceanfront project in Bargara, and Furtado Property's recently lodged 113-apartment residential proposal in West End, Brisbane.

His public development approach places strong emphasis on location, enduring design, quality delivery and the experience of the people who will ultimately live in each project.
