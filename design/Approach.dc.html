Approach — Graham Furtado
Approach

From the right site to the right outcome.

A development philosophy built on finding the right site, quality design, experienced architects and designers, lasting workmanship, and responsibility for the complete development journey.

Six principles · One journey
01Site

Finding the right opportunity.

Every project begins with the process of finding the right site before anything is progressed. Location, context and the potential a site holds are considered first, because the quality of the opportunity shapes everything that follows.

02Strategy

Understanding the market before shaping the product.

Market fit, location, target market and development direction are settled early. Long-term real-estate experience informs the project direction, product positioning and the residential offer, so the development answers a real gap rather than assuming one.

03Design

Creating places designed to remain relevant.

Furtado Property partners with experienced architects and designers and focuses on lasting workmanship and design. The intent is buildings that remain relevant over time and make an architectural contribution to their local community.

04Coordination

Bringing the right specialists together.

Architects, designers, planners, engineers, builders, sales teams and other specialists are coordinated as one team, with the development vision and the end-user outcome held constant throughout.

05Delivery

Turning development intent into a finished residential outcome.

The process runs from site identification through to handing over the keys. Responsibility does not stop at approval or at construction; it continues through construction, market delivery and the quality of the finished home.

06People

Designing around how people ultimately live.

Project material emphasises the quality of the finished home and how people will live in it, not only the building as an asset. Residential environments are shaped around the experience of the people who ultimately occupy them.
